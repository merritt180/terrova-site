Local SEO for Med Spas: How to Get Found by Clients Ready to Book

Booking a facial, a round of filler, or a laser treatment almost always starts the same way. Someone opens Google, types "med spa near me" or "Botox in Sherman Oaks," and looks at the three businesses on the map. Whoever sits at the top of that list usually wins the appointment, and it is rarely the spa with the best treatments. It is the spa that did the local SEO work.

Med spas live and die by local search. Your clients are not driving across the city to find you. They want a provider they trust close to home, and they decide in the few minutes they spend scrolling Google on their phone. The spa that shows up first, looks complete, and has fresh reviews earns the call. Here is how to become that spa.

Start with a complete Google Business Profile

Your Google Business Profile is the storefront most new clients see before they ever reach your website. For a med spa, a bare profile is a real liability, because clients are spending money on their face and their body and they want proof you are established and professional. Fill in every field: hours, phone, booking link, a full description, and your complete service list. Add real photos of your space, your team, and your results where you are permitted to show them.

Why it matters: a complete profile with strong photos and a full menu of treatments earns far more clicks than a listing that shows only a name and a number. Most profiles we audit are less than half finished, which means the work to pull ahead is often already sitting in front of you.

Choose categories that match how clients search

Google decides which searches you are eligible to appear in largely based on your categories, and this is where many med spas quietly lose ground. If your primary category is set to "Spa" instead of "Medical Spa," you may never surface when someone searches for injectables or laser treatments. Set your primary category to the one that best describes your core business, then add secondary categories for the specific services you offer, such as "Skin Care Clinic" or "Laser Hair Removal Service."

The fix: match your categories to the exact treatments you want to book. The more accurately they line up with real searches, the more often the right clients find you.

Turn reviews into your quiet engine

For a med spa, reviews do double duty. They help you rank, and they calm the nerves of someone deciding whether to trust you with a needle or a laser. A spa with 30 recent, detailed reviews will almost always beat a spa with 60 reviews that stopped two years ago. Google rewards recent activity, and clients read the newest reviews first.

The fix: build a simple habit of asking every happy client for a review before they leave, with a direct link you can text them on the spot. The best moment is right after they see a result they love. A client glowing about their skin at the front desk is far more likely to follow through than one you email three days later.

Put your treatments and your city in plain text

A lot of med spa websites are beautiful and say almost nothing Google can use. Soft phrases like "reveal your best self" look elegant, but they give Google nothing to match against a real search. If your pages never plainly state what you offer and where you offer it, Google has to guess, and it guesses conservatively.

The fix: make sure your treatments and your city appear in your page titles, headings, and body text in plain language. "Botox, dermal fillers, and laser facials in Pasadena" tells Google exactly who to show you to. This is often a small edit to pages you already have, with an outsized effect on how often you appear.

Keep your name, address, and phone consistent everywhere

Google cross-checks your business details against directories like Yelp and health and beauty listings across the web. If your address is written three different ways, or an old suite number or phone line is still floating around, Google trusts your listing less and your ranking suffers for it.

The fix: pick one exact format for your name, address, and phone number, then update every listing to match. It is tedious work, but it is one of the most reliable ways to climb in the map results.

Stay visible after setup

Google favors businesses that stay active, and med spas have a natural advantage here because there is always something to share. New treatments, seasonal offers, results you are proud of, and team updates all give you something to post. A profile that gets a fresh post and a few new photos every couple of weeks signals a business that is thriving. A profile that goes quiet for months slowly drifts down the map.

The fix: spend 20 focused minutes a week posting an update, adding a photo, and replying to every new review, good or bad. Consistency beats intensity here.

The bottom line

Clients choosing a med spa are making a personal, high trust decision, and most of them make it on Google before they ever pick up the phone. You win that decision by being complete, being reviewed, being clear about your treatments and location, and staying active. None of this takes a big budget. It takes doing the basics consistently while your competitors leave their profiles half finished. Most med spas that commit to these steps see real movement within 60 to 90 days.
